MathSAT5 is covered under a restrictive license. Linking smt-switch against it
alters the license of smt-switch. If you choose to do this, you must obtain the
library yourself and ensure you meet all the license requirements.

Download MathSAT from https://mathsat.fbk.eu/download.html. Unpack the archive into
./deps and rename the resulting toplevel directory to mathsat, so that the headers
are at ./deps/mathsat/include and the library at ./deps/mathsat/lib, which is where
smt-switch looks by default.

If you would rather keep it somewhere else, leave it where it is and point
smt-switch at it instead:

./configure.sh --msat --msat-dir=/path/to/mathsat

You should now be able to build smt-switch with MathSAT5 as a backend solver by
configuring smt-switch with the --msat flag.
